Open TitleScreenMixin.java.

PARALLAX:
- `zoom = 1.035f` controls how much extra image is rendered outside the screen.
  Higher values allow more camera movement, but can crop more of the artwork.
- `driftX` and `driftY` control the automatic camera drift.
  Lower multipliers make it slower and calmer.
- `mouseParallaxX` and `mouseParallaxY` control the tiny mouse-follow effect.
  Set their multipliers to 0 for a purely automatic camera.

OTHER ANIMATION:
- The particle count is the `for (int i = 0; i < 18; i++)` loop.
- Lower 18 to 10 or 8 for weaker phones.
